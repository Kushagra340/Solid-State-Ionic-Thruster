# ⚠️ High-Voltage Laboratory Safety Protocols

1. **Dielectric Clearance**: Maintain minimum 100mm clearance from all energized 30kV conductors.
2. **Grounding Discharge Wand**: Always discharge capacitors and electrodes to earth ground using an insulated 100kΩ discharge wand prior to touching the apparatus.
3. **Ozone ($O_3$) Mitigation**: Corona discharge produces toxic ozone and nitrogen oxides ($NO_x$). Operate strictly in ventilated fume hoods or outdoor testing environments.
4. **PPE**: Use 35kV-rated dielectric lineworker gloves and polycarbonate eye protection.
